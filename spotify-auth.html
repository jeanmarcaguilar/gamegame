🎵 Spotify Authentication

Connect your Spotify account to display your currently playing music on your portfolio.

⚠️ Setup Required:

  1. Create an app at https://developer.spotify.com/dashboard
  2. Add this redirect URI to your Spotify app:
  3. Enter your Client ID below
  4. Click "Connect to Spotify" and authorize the app
  5. Copy the refresh token and add it to your .env file

✅ Authentication Successful!

Your Spotify account is now connected.

Next Steps:

  1. Copy the refresh token above
  2. Add it to your .env file as VITE_SPOTIFY_REFRESH_TOKEN
  3. Make sure you also have VITE_SPOTIFY_CLIENT_ID set
  4. Restart your development server
